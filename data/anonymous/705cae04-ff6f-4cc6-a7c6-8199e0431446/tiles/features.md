# Features


## Tiles
Tiles are the sections of your story. They appear in the left panel and are all rendered together in the preview pane, in order.


* **Drag** tiles in the panel to reorder them
* **Double-click** in the editor to scroll the preview to the same position
* **Double-click** in the preview to jump to that tile in the editor
* **Right-click** in the editor to insert a table, a picture, or a ‡keyword


## Highlights
Highlights track recurring story elements — characters, places, objects, themes.


* Each highlight shows a count of how many times it is mentioned across all tiles
* **Renaming** a highlight renames all of its occurrences in the story automatically
* Lines starting with **‡** inside a highlight become keyword tags in the left panel
* Click a keyword tag to group all highlights sharing that keyword at the top
* Sort highlights alphabetically or by occurrence count (most → least or least → most)


## Todos
Create interactive checkboxes anywhere in a tile with `- [ ]` syntax.


* The left panel shows unchecked / total count across all tiles
* Click a checkbox directly in the panel to toggle it
* Click the tile name next to a todo to jump to it in the editor


## Filter
The search bar in the header filters everything in real time.


* In the story list: shows only stories whose name or tile content matches
* Inside a story: shows only the matching tiles and highlights
* Matching text is highlighted in the panel


## Stats (footer)
The footer shows live word and character counts:


* **Words / Chars** — current tile, then total across all tiles
* **Pages** — estimated at 1 page for 300 words (1 minute read)


## Speech to text
In Chrome, click the **microphone** icon in the header to dictate into the editor. Interim results appear as ghost text; the transcript is inserted when you pause.


## Publishing
Click the **Publish** button in the header to make your story publicly readable via a shareable URL, without requiring a login (HOSTED mode only).


## Highlights toggle
Click the **Highlights** button in the header to show or hide highlight colouring in the preview pane.

# Features
## Tiles
* Tiles can be re-ordered by dragging them (in the left menu)
* All tiles, in order, are shown in the rendering pane
* double click somewhere on the text to auto-scroll the rendering page to the same line
* double click somewhere on the rendering pane to auto-scroll the text to the same line
  * (this will jump to the proper tile)
## Highlights
* Highlights can be used to manage story elements, such as characters.
* Each highlight shows the number of times it is mentioned in the story
* Renaming a highlight renames all of its occurrences in the story
* Keywords in highlights appear on the left menu
* Click on a keyword in the left menu to group all similar highlights at the top
* Highlights can be ordered in alphabetical order, or by order of occurences (most to least, least to most)
## Misc - header
* If you use neowwriter via Chrome, click the mike icon in the header to enable speech-to-text
* Press the highlight button to toggle highlight... highlighting ON or OFF
## Misc - footer
* On the footer on the left, you can see:
  * Number of words (current tile /all tiles)
  * Number of characters (current tile / all tiles)
  * Number of pages / minutes to read (300 words for one page)
## Others
* TODOs are collected on the left menu (unchecked / total)
* You can complete them there by clicking on the box
* Or go to the tile where the TODO lies by clicking on the tile name at the end of the TODO line there

# Highlight colours

Highlights track recurring story elements — characters, places, objects, themes.
Each highlight name is assigned one of **28 colours** automatically and consistently:
7 colour families (blue, red, yellow, green, purple, orange, grey), each with
4 typographic variants (normal, bold, italic, bold italic) so no two highlights look the same.

Toggle the **Highlights** button in the header to show or hide the colouring in the preview.
